👨‍🔬 Hamza Azam · Identity & Doctoral Research

ZHEJIANG UNIVERSITY · NINGBO GLOBAL INNOVATION CENTER
I work at the convergence of biomechatronics, musculoskeletal modeling, and applied machine learning. My research solves a foundational bottleneck in wearable robotics: inferring unobservable internal joint kinetics and musculoskeletal states directly from on-body sensors and wearable joint encoders outside laboratory confines, and using these representations to drive adaptive, patient-compliant exoskeletons.

Unlike conventional laboratory gait analysis that requires multi-camera optical motion capture arrays and stationary ground reaction force plates, my doctoral work develops continuous, subject-independent estimators that run in real-time on wearable edge hardware. By pairing non-linear state mapping with dynamic limit-cycle control (Hm-DMP), our exoskeletons synchronize with the wearer's voluntary cadence without fighting biological limb trajectories.
